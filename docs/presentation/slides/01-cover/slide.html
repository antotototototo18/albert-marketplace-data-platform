Group 6 · Anthony, Antoine, Benjamin, Colin, Lenny

ANTOINE, 1 min. Good morning. We are group 6, and we built a data platform for Albert's Marketplace, an online shop. When we started, the shop had a few problems. First, the website and the analysts used the same database, so a big analysis could slow down customers at checkout. Second, nobody agreed on revenue. The old export writes every order twice. If you add everything up, you get 39.2 million euros. The real figure is 19.6 million. Third, the data science team wanted one simple table: units sold per product, per country, per day. No table gave them that. And on our shared cloud project, anyone could read 200,001 customer phone numbers. Nobody had decided that, it just happened. HANDOVER: Colin will show you what we built to fix this.
